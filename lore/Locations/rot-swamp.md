# Rot Swamp

Drowned trees and sucking mud. Hunger runes surface here, and the Ford-holder's prison lies at the drowned ford.

## Canon

- **Drowned trees, reeds, sucking mud that slows the walker. Drowned chests ("Only black water.").** (seen in: biomes.js, chest loot)
- **Hunger runes surface here more often; metal found is mostly bronze.** (seen in: biomes.js)
- **The Ford-holder's** Crimson stone ties to the drowned ford (round 31).
