# The Battlefield

A field never cleared. The dead of the long war still lie where they fell, among stuck weapons, stakes and gibbets. Most Hollows walk here.

## Canon

- **Weapons stuck in the earth, corpses, scattered remains, shields, impaling stakes, gibbets, faded banners, abandoned carts and crates, runestones.** (seen in: biomes.js, props)
- **Faded banners:** their description says the words "Not him" were stitched there once. (design canon, round 27; not built)
- **Shards lie wherever the war was fought** (round 29).
- **The Paymaster's** Crimson stone ties here, and to the Wanderer's past (round 31).
- Favours no rune type today (biomes.js).

## Open

- **Carts and crates as gleaners' carts** (wire coils, pliers, burst satchels). (round 28 proposal)
- **Guard line on the field:** "Nobody burned them. Nobody had the time." (round 14 proposal)
- **Break** (and Sting second) as root names favoured here. See [Root names](../Runes/root-names.md).
- **The shriving pair, a prop** (round 39, the user's idea: "the body of a man and a monk in the middle of the shriving, both cut down"; details below are the loremaster's proposal, not decided):
  - The dying soldier lies on his back, both hands closed on his chest round a stone; a knotted rite cord at his wrist. The chaplain knelt at his side and fell forward across him, cut from behind, so the two bodies make a low X. The chaplain's hands are empty and open ("A shriving hand holds no stone").
  - The stone is a keeper's blank, grey, unwired and **not glowing**: the chaplain was cut down before he said the name, so nothing went in. (A finished stone would be wired by gleaners and glow at its tier; this one is neither.)
  - Battlefield biome, rare. Not the prologue's dead man: that stone must be a real wired rune to be read.
  - Proposed description: "A chaplain and the man he knelt for. The rite was not finished." On the stone: "A blank. Nothing in it."

## Rejected

- **A headstone prop** (round 39). Rejected round 39: the dead are burned, not buried, so there are no headstones. Replaced by the shriving pair.
