# Wayside Shrines

Roadside altars of the good gods, where a rune is given back and its soul freed. Wend's road ends here. Freeing a soul here is the only thing that lowers corruption.

## Canon

- **Shrines of the good gods by the roads, where runes are given back and souls freed.** (round 8, design canon)
- **They are Wend's.** In the good death, kin carried the ashes here, set them down and turned back without looking. (round 18)
- **Rites here:** Wend "Leave him by the road" / Mortmain "Lay him in the niche" (an iron grate closes; the stone stays visible forever). (round 19)
- **Only Wend's rite lowers corruption.** (round 20)
- **A found shrine stays where it was** in the saved world, even when the Whisper makes it look like rubble. (round 10)
- **Crimson Release** happens here; a released jailer leaves a small fire that never goes out (firelight, not rune light). (round 31)
- **"Milestones"** on the old roads carry "Not him" and the Circle's mark (rounds 27, 31).
