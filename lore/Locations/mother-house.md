# The Mother House

The Order's seat, where stones were graded, logged and sold to the crowns. Now fallen, abandoned and forgotten: no one remembers where it stands. The late-game place, with old keepers in the undercroft.

## Canon (round 28, design canon, not built)

- **The Order's seat,** where stones were graded, entered in the master ledgers and sold to the crowns.
- **Fallen and abandoned after the war, and forgotten (the Greying):** no one remembers where it stands, so it is found through ledger clues (the first keeper's sale-book page, the champion's sealing, torn chapel pages).
- **Old keepers hold its undercroft.**
- **The late-game place,** the "apostate cathedral" of ART_RULES §6.
- **It certified the king's echo** and sold certified readings; its mark is stamped on the champion's gauntlet. (rounds 27, 31)
- **The court keeper**, last Crimson prisoner, ties here (round 31).
