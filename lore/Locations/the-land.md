# The Land

A small kingdom at a crossroads of crowns, where the whole war was fought. It is grey, dead and unburied, and goes on with no edge: the neighbours' lands are just as ruined and forgotten, so there is nowhere to escape to. Its old name has been forgotten.

## Canon

- **The present age:** the world is ruined and has already lost. Kingdoms are broken, battlefields never cleared, the dead lie where they fell. (seen in: ART_RULES §1, §6; Battlefield props)
- **It is desaturated and dead.** The only vivid color and true light comes from runes; firelight is the only other light. (seen in: ART_RULES §1, §3) Why: [the Greying](../Gods/hush.md).
- **A war is within living memory.** Sons went to the battlefield and didn't return. (claim, peasant: "My son went to the battlefield. The crows came back.")
- **Hunger and scarcity.** (claim, peasant: "The crows eat better than we do.")
- **Procedural:** biomes are large regions; villages are rare. (seen in: world.js, CLAUDE.md) Areas are to be saved (see [README](../README.md#ground-rules)).
- **LA + LB, a small kingdom at a crossroads of crowns** (round 34): the heirs split it and the neighbours poured in, so the whole war was fought on it. **No hard edge:** the world goes on, and the neighbours' lands are as ruined and forgotten, so there is no "away".
- **TB, the war's long tail** (round 34): "now" = year 0; see the [Timeline](../Timeline/the-testament-war.md).

## Open (round 32 proposals)

- **The name:** the kingdom's name greyed with its king (offered to Hush). **The old name is deliberately withheld.** People use a plain folk name; candidates: **the Wake**, the Long Field, the Unburied.
- **Reckoning (recommended):** years were counted by kings' reigns ("the ninth year of..."), and the reigns greyed with the crowns, so **no one can say the year.** Each village counts its own winters since its gate shut (no common standard, like barter rates). Only chapel ledgers keep a running count (Ennick's count), so ledger dates are the one way to order events. Line: "Twelve winters since we shut the gate. Ask the monk for the year. He'll have it in a book."
- **Real-world feel (recommended):** late-medieval arms (plate, greatswords, pikes, **no gunpowder**); a war shaped like the Thirty Years' War (a generation long, hired companies changing sides, debased coin, hunger); Viking-age money (hacksilver, peck-marked coins, everything weighed); a cold north-eastern land (log stockades, bog, forest, snow) like the Baltic crusade country, with a monastic order that sold to crowns. Kept apart from Berserk's Midland (castles and courts, Hundred Years' War chivalry, a ruling Holy See).
- **The Mother House at the crossroads:** the Order put it there because a crossroads let it sell to every crown. (proposal)
- **Procedural layout:** each world shuffles the biomes, so lore can never say "the swamp lies south of the pass". Places are kinds, not fixed spots; authored places (Mother House, court chapel) need placement rules.

## Rejected

- **TA, ten years after** (war over 9–10 years ago; Wanderer ~50). Not chosen round 34: what did he do for ten years?
- **TC, long after** (20+ years). Not recommended: the war leaves living memory, and the keeper's ten years and the jailer's eleven would change.
- **LC, a closed basin** ringed by mountains, the Frozen Pass the only way out. Not chosen: needs a finite world with an edge.
