# Timeline: After the War (Now)

The last three years and the present. The Order is broken, Hollows walk, villages have shut their gates, and the dark monks hide among the rest. The game is played here, after a prologue at the war's end.

## Canon

1. **~3 years ago to now: the ruined age.** The order is broken, Hollows walk, villages wall themselves in. The dark monks hide among the rest. (rounds 4, 7)
   - **The Wanderer's three years** (round 38): woken from madness by a name he spoke, he gives the long rite, without haste and without a blank, to the dying he meets; first from guilt, then for peace. At the Pull each costs blood. His corruption falls slowly from 100% to 25–35%. He barely survives the withdrawal and comes out thin and worn. (round 38b) See [The Wanderer](../Characters/the-wanderer.md).
2. **The Mother House** lies fallen and forgotten, old keepers in its undercroft. (round 28)
3. **The first keeper** has been ten years alone at the Pull in his chapel. (round 29)
4. **A handful of Circle members are left,** taken for ramblers; their jailers still hold the doors. (round 35)
5. **Year 0: the game cuts here** to a village, after the prologue at the war's end. The Wanderer is about 45, at 25–35% corruption. (rounds 9, 34, 38)
