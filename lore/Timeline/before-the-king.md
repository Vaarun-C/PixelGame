# Timeline: Before the King

From the pious age to the testament king's youth. The dark gods hide, then grow; some monks turn and start trapping souls in stones, which sit in reliquaries for generations before anyone uses them. "Now" = year 0.

## Canon

1. **Ancient times.** All the gods already exist. The world is pious; the good death (the Naming, the Letting Go, the Walking) frees the dead to the good gods. The dark gods are weak and hide, each inside one part of the rite: Hush in the silence after the name, Grudge in the grief thrown on the pyre, Mortmain in the keepsake. (rounds 6, 8, 18)
2. **Wrongdoing slowly strengthens the dark gods; they corrupt some monks** (the keepers). (rounds 6–7)
3. **About 120 years ago: the false rite begins.** Mortmain teaches the first turned monks his craft: the keeper-made blank and the withholding rite (the name spoken, the release left out). Souls go into stones; the reliquaries fill. Hush eats what is later offered. (rounds 18–19, 34, 36)
4. **The relic doctrine:** the Order calls the stones harmless relics. (round 17; exact start is open, see below)
5. **About a lifetime before the war:** the future testament king hears the true long rite at his father's death. (rounds 27, 32) The true rite was still given then.
