# Timeline: The Testament War

About 31 to 3 years ago. A generation-long war over two words, fought on the dead king's land by his heirs and their neighbours. Chaplains harvested souls on every field, the dead rose, the money turned to iron, and the war simply ran out. "Now" = year 0 (TB, round 34).

## Canon

1. **~31 years ago: "Not him" is certified as the king's will.** The heirs, the king's two sons (round 38d), split the kingdom and go to war; neighbouring crowns back one heir or another for land and switch sides. (round 27)
2. **Early in the war: the readings of the king,** the first atrocity. The king's champion socketed his Rend stone and cut down prisoners before the lords until it read. (round 27) (*Round 39 flag:* "socketed" kept until the user decides whether smithed settings existed; elsewhere canon now says "lashed".)
3. **Through the war:** war chaplains give the field form on the battlefields, with keeper-made blanks gripped by the dying (the Wanderer among them, from ~23 years ago, unknowing). The crown or company pays the keepers for the blanks and hands them to the chaplains with their wages; captains take them for prayer stones. Gleaners wire and cart the stones; the Mother House grades and sells them; the crowns buy finished stones with silver (weapons, war chests, ransoms) and the trade drives more trapping. Wages stay coin. (rounds 9, 16, 28, 36, 38b)
3a. **Through the war (undated): both sons die.** Each claim passes on (to his sons, a regent, or captains fighting in his name), and the war becomes about the sentence more than the men. (round 38e)
4. **Through the war:** stones broken unspoken by the thousand loose souls into the fields' dead; **the dead rise.** More men earn "Not yet" over and over and become Unfallen; the Circle keeps sealing them. (rounds 10, 30, 37)
5. **Background: hunger.** Years of blight and failed harvests make men cheap and crowns hungry. Not the cause. (round 27)
6. **Late in the war (undated): someone offers the king's stone to Hush.** He is forgotten; only the words survive, so no peace is possible. (round 27; who is withheld)
7. **~11 years ago: the silver runs out; the king's champion is sealed** by the widow of a man he cut. (rounds 31, 34) With no silver, the crowns can no longer buy finished stones. (round 38b)
8. **~10 years ago: crowns stop buying stones; the chapels fall** (keepers rest the stock and leave with the sale book); **wire money is struck.** The first keeper is sealed in his chapel. (rounds 22, 29, 34)
   - **From here to the war's end the keepers hand out blanks for almost nothing,** because they need the trapping, not the metal: every trapping feeds Mortmain on its own. Supply never runs out; chaplains keep shriving with blanks to the last day (the Wanderer among them). (rounds 38, 38b)
   - **Finished stones have no buyer.** They pile up in chapel naves and lie where they fell on the fields; why the world is full of runes. **The Paymaster alone pays his men in stones.** (round 38b)
9. **~8 to 4 years ago: the silver wash wears through; pay breaks.** Companies desert and starve, fields go unfarmed, the dead outrun the living (stones broken unspoken, round 10), more Unfallen are sealed. (rounds 22, 26, 34)
10. **~3 years ago: the last bands stop fighting,** on no date anyone agrees on. No victory, no treaty; it ran out. The crowns fade from memory. (rounds 26–27, 34) **The Wanderer is at the Pull,** mad from thousands of trappings; he crossed into the Whisper late and hit the Pull on or near the last field (round 38b). Starving from withdrawal, on a field he reads a wired Pale Rend taken from a dead man, and a name he spoke comes back. This is the game's playable prologue. (rounds 38, 38b)

## Open

- **Ten and eleven don't clash** (round 32 note): the first keeper counts from his chapel's fall, the widow from her sealing, so the champion was sealed a year before that chapel fell.
- **Facts that fix time** (round 32): the war lasted a generation and is in living memory; the king heard the long rite at his father's death; wire money came late and was found out by handling; coin faces are crisp; the Wanderer trained beside the Paymaster's chaplain.
- **When and how each son died** is unstated (round 38e). Order against the king's stone being offered is open too.
- **"Late in the war"** for the silver running out is year 20 of 28: a loose fit. (history point 12)
- **When the Mother House fell** and **how the crowns and heirs were forgotten:** see [Mother House](../Locations/mother-house.md), [The crowns and heirs](../Organizations/the-crowns-and-heirs.md).

## Rejected

- **Blanks running short late in the war** (round 36 proposal). Rejected round 38: supply had no reason to run out; the captains carrying on fed the Quiet gods. See [Blanks](../Runes/blanks.md).
- **Stones become company pay** (round 38). Replaced round 38b: wages stayed coin, which is why the wash wearing through broke pay; only the Paymaster paid in stones.
