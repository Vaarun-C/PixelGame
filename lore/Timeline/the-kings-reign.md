# Timeline: The King's Reign

The testament king grows rich on beast stones, fights jealous neighbours with stones of men, and loses his inner circle when they learn the truth. His reign has no fixed dates; it ends with his death about 31 years ago.

## Canon

1. **The king finds the keepers hidden in the Order and becomes their patron.** No pact; he feeds the dark gods through their rites and boons follow. (round 34)
2. **His inner circle of readers** (some from the Order) makes and runs the first tool runes: beast stones in yokes, ploughs and churns. The kingdom grows rich; **the gold heads are struck.** (rounds 34, 37)
3. **The real beasts are used up;** what ate them starves; only the crows do well. (round 34)
4. **Border wars:** rivals are jealous of his prosperity. His champions carry prisoners' stones: **his kingdom is the first to wield men's stones.** He gets "Not yet" from Grudge and quiets his stones by offering to Hush. (rounds 34, 37)
5. **Using leaks, making stays secret:** rivals read a few seized stones but can't make more. (round 36)
6. **His inner circle learns the truth** when he begins making runes from prisoners of war. **They turn the craft on the strongest of his guards,** maddened and overpowered by their runes, and seal them in Crimson: **the first Crimson stones and the start of the Crimson Circle.** Many first members give themselves as jailers. (rounds 35, 37)
7. **The king's court begins to die** (corruption wears him down, short of the Pull). (round 34)
8. **The keepers leave his dying court** for the Order's houses and begin selling stones (and certified readers) to every crown, which buy at once. One keeper stays. (rounds 35, 36)
9. **About 31 years ago: the king dies at home.** He asks for the long rite; his court keeper gives the field form. "Not him." (rounds 27, 34)
