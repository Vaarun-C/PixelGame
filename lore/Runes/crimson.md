# Crimson Runes

A Crimson rune is a prison. A willing person went into the stone and holds the door shut on a man who could not be killed (one of the [Unfallen](../Characters/the-unfallen.md)). The Wanderer can wear it, burn it for a huge spell, or free the jailer at a shrine and fight the prisoner.

## Canon

### What it is (rounds 3–4, 8, 30)

- **A prison made by sacrifice.** A willing person's life seals something too strong to defeat. The sacrifice could be anyone, but must be willing. (design canon)
- **It holds two names:** the sacrifice's and the prisoner's.
- **Every Crimson rune is unique,** with its own sprite for who was sacrificed and what is imprisoned. (design canon; today Crimson is a generic tier, see runes.js)
- **Why the seal needed a willing soul** (round 30): binding the living needs consent or the living binding, a dark rite. The prisoner would never consent and the circle would not use a dark rite, so a willing soul went in first and gave the yes the prisoner wouldn't: **two names, one yes.** **The sacrifice is the jailer,** holding the door shut from inside forever.
- **The downside comes from the prisoner** (his will, his weight), not from Mortmain's grip; the jailer is willing. (round 37)
- **Buff and cost are his strength and his burden:** Crimson Rend, his wounds that never closed (you are hurt more); Hunger, his hunger (you starve); Ward, the weight of one who would not fall (you move slower). (fits: runes.js)
- **Current costs in game** (seen in: runes.js): Rend, hurt more by every blow; Hunger, out of a fight you starve (lose health slowly); Ward, you move slower. Cursed resonances hit twice as hard; cursed Flay bleeds you too; cursed Gorge refuses all healing but its own; other cursed resonances make you take more damage.
- **The look:** weeping cracks, bone or flesh growing into it, glyph strokes uneven. (seen in: ART_RULES §5)
- **Never goes quiet** under Hush's favour (round 12): its downside is the prisoner's will, not a soul remembering its name (round 37).
- **Names:** the sacrifice's name is learned by bearing the lashed rune and its downside (reading). (round 5) The prisoner's true name is pieced together from 2–3 places: chapel ledgers, monks, and the sacrifice's grave or kin; never from the prisoner. (round 5) (*Round 39 flag:* the dead are burned and there are no headstones, so "grave" needs a reword, e.g. his pyre place or kin.) The circle tore each name into pieces and hid them in different ledgers. (round 30)
- A Crimson rune can be carried and lashed for its whole life without ever being uttered or released.

### What the Wanderer can do (round 30, the user's design; a reward, not a punishment)

1. **Lash** (was "Socket", renamed round 39): a big upside with a big downside. (built, as a socket)
2. **Utter:** a huge spell. It releases the prisoner to be fought, **consumes the jailer**, and adds a lot of corruption. You must know both names (round 5).
3. **Offer: impossible.** No god will take something so horrendous. Why (round 31): a god receives the dead, and he cannot die; there is nothing in him to take, eat or keep, and Grudge, who owns him, will not let go. Text: "The lid won't close. Nothing down there wants him."
4. **Release:** frees the jailer and lets the prisoner out to be fought. Needs both names, done at a [wayside shrine](../Locations/wayside-shrines.md) (Wend's road, where the jailer's good death ends); lowers corruption more than an ordinary freeing. (round 31)

### On either path (round 31)

- **No body to hunt:** the prisoner rises at once in the nearest dead body where he is let out. His old body dies at that moment.
- **One "Not yet":** his first killing blow leaves him standing once; then he dies.
- **Forced stones he carried drop** as souls to free.

### The Release reward (round 31)

1. **His gear is proof and tool:** one unique piece per prisoner (the champion's reading gauntlet, see [The Unfallen](../Characters/the-unfallen.md)).
2. **The jailer's parting echo:** she was willing, so she speaks her own name, why she chose the stone, and one secret the circle kept.
3. **A lasting mark:** a small fire that never goes out at that shrine (firelight, not rune light), and her kin in a village who now speak of her in peace (their lines change).

### Uttering Crimson (round 31)

- A huge spell per type that **also wounds the prisoner as he rises,** so the fight after is easier: a sure win, anywhere, now.
  - **Rend:** one cut that doesn't stop, a wide sweep through everything near, armour or not.
  - **Ward: nothing can move, ever.** Every living or walking thing near is held fast for good, like a statue. Ward is holding, Mortmain's craft. **The held stay in the saved world as grey statue props** (search text: "It was walking once. It will not again."); held loose-soul Hollows stay held. **The rising prisoner is not held;** he rises after the spell.
  - **Hunger:** he drinks every walking thing in sight and is made whole.
- **He hears the jailer's echo one last time, cut off mid-word;** the crimson goes out from the inside. Proposed text: "She held the door for eleven years. You opened it with her." This must be a per-stone string (jailers were sealed at different times). (round 32 note)
- **No hatred Hollow rises:** the prisoner is what rises.
- **Cost:** the most corruption of any act except the living binding. None of Release's rewards; he keeps the spell and the prisoner's gear.

### Originality rules (round 4; keeps clear of Berserk's Behelit, ART_RULES §10)

- No faces on the stones.
- **The player never performs the sacrifice.** All sacrifices are in the past. So the Wanderer can't end the game by becoming one.
- The released thing never offers deals, bargains or gifts. It is a thing to fight.

## Open

- Whether a released Crimson rune can be rebuilt (presumably not; the prison is gone).
- Whether Crimson runes can be carved. (Fusing settled round 42: never; jailer and prisoner already make two souls, "There's no room in a Crimson stone." See [Fusing](fusing.md).)
- Unique Crimson sprites need an art-rule update: the icon sheet is rows = type, cols = tier (ART_RULES §13).
- **Reserved, not rejected: (F) true whispers** (an offering lets one true thing through: a name, a ledger line, a Crimson piece). Risks (round 11): at 75%+ it mixes unmarked with the Whisper's lies (eased since favour text is always true, round 13, but a whispered name is a fact, not a favour sign); it could shortcut the name hunt; it echoes Bloodborne's Insight.

## Rejected

- **Speaking as the only way out, always destroying the sacrifice** (round 8). Superseded round 30: Release frees the jailer; only Utter consumes her.
- **A shrine frees the sacrifice but breaks the prison anyway** (round 8 proposal). Folded into Release (round 30).
- **Crimson sacrifices only soldiers; Crimson as the one rune with no soldier** (round 3). Rejected round 4: anyone, and willing.
