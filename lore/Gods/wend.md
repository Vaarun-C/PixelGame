# Wend, the Road Home

The good goddess of the Walking. She receives the freed dead at her wayside shrines. Only her rite lowers corruption. Her dark shadow is [Mortmain](mortmain.md).

## Canon

- **Wend, the Road Home** (round 18). Receives the freed dead; the [wayside shrines](../Locations/wayside-shrines.md) are hers. Patient, footsore; waits at the roadside until the last soul arrives.
- **Her rite:** at the road shrine: "Leave him by the road." The dark rite there is Mortmain's "Lay him in the niche". (round 19)
- **Only Wend's rite lowers corruption,** because only it finishes a good death. (round 20)
- **Wend never asks a fee: the road takes the coinless dead too.** The poor only fear it doesn't. (round 23; see [Currency](../Mechanics/currency-and-barter.md))
- **She sends the Wanderer back after death while his corruption is low** (round 21). From the Whisper on, the dark gods do it (round 10). **The road carries only the man** (round 24): his bag stays where he fell. See [Death](../Mechanics/death.md).
- **Release of a Crimson rune is done at a wayside shrine,** where the jailer's good death ends. (round 31)
