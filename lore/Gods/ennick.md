# Ennick of the Knots

The good god of the Naming. He keeps count of every name said at a deathbed. Cords and ledgers are his signs. His dark shadow is [Hush](hush.md).

## Canon

- **Ennick of the Knots** (round 18). Keeps the count of every name said at a deathbed; rite cords and chapel ledgers are his signs. Exact, and unforgiving of a lie.
- **With his last strength he protects names.** That is why clue names never lie, even in the Whisper (round 15 rule, given a cause in round 18). Narrowed round 20: this covers names only; a soul's echo (its wish) can be misheard in the Whisper.
- **His rite:** at the ledger in the Chapel Ruins: "Write his name." (round 19) The dark rite at the same place is Hush's "Scrape his name". See [The six rites](../Mechanics/the-six-rites.md).
- **His buff** (round 21): for a while after his step is done, a lie about a name shows struck through. Practical, never combat power.
- His step finishes a part of the death; it gives no corruption drop. The soul goes free only at Wend's road. (round 20)

## Open
