# Hush, Who Eats the Name

The dark shadow of [Ennick](ennick.md). He eats names: what is offered to him is forgotten, even by the dead man's own family. The world's grey, the Greying, is his. He does not make stones or hold souls; that is [Mortmain](mortmain.md)'s work.

## Canon

- **Hush, Who Eats the Name** (round 18). Ennick's shadow. Hid in the silence after the name was said.
- **Hush only consumes** (round 36). He eats what is offered and gives the silence and the forgetting. **He does not make stones or hold souls in them.** (Replaces round 19's "in that silence Hush takes the soul into the stone"; the holding is Mortmain's.)
- **He feeds only on names** (round 37). He is fed when stones are offered to him, which is what the keepers did (the Chapel Ruins "resting", the king's stone).
- **An offered name is forgotten, even by kin.** (rounds 12, 17)
- **His rite:** the offering (round 12), also "Scrape his name" at the ledger (round 19). See [Offering and favour](../Mechanics/offering-and-favour.md).
- **His favour: the stones go quiet** (rounds 12, 21; re-grounded round 37). When the Wanderer offers a rune, Hush also tastes the names of the forced souls he carries. For a while they forget who they were, so they stop straining against Mortmain's grip, and their downsides ease. Details in [Offering and favour](../Mechanics/offering-and-favour.md).
- **The old keepers' Hush boon:** their own stones always quiet, and a face no one remembers. (round 28)
- **Hush and Mortmain together** (round 36): Mortmain fills the stones (blank and withholding rite); Hush empties them (eats what is offered, brings the forgetting). The first turned monks served both.

### The Greying (name round 16; meaning G1 "Forgetting", round 17)

- **Color is what the living remember of a thing.**
- **An offered name is forgotten, even by kin; what no one remembers greys.** The offered stone greying is the same thing in small.
- **Rune color is the last of what is still remembered.** That is why runes are the only vivid color left. (fits: ART_RULES §1, §3)
- **It crept in with the offerings.** (round 17; the one-stroke Vault Night, G2, was rejected round 24)
- **Grey huts, wells and faces are not lore** (round 24): wood and stone are grey; it is the asset palette, not the Greying.
- **Coin faces are crisp but unremembered** (round 24); the gold heads carry the [testament king](../Characters/the-testament-king.md)'s face, offered to Hush (round 29).

## Open

- **Greying endings** (round 16 proposal): the grey never lifts; the best ending stops the forgetting, not the grey. Round 40 proposals build on this (the master ledger carried out vs scraped); see [The Wanderer](../Characters/the-wanderer.md).
- **Art note:** nothing restores color in play (ART_RULES §1, §3). Any ending that shows color needs an ART_RULES exception.
- **Whisper owner:** the Whisper fits Hush under G1, as a reading only; madness stays unowned. (round 18)
- **The Greying hid itself** (round 25 proposal): no one notices a forgotten name.
