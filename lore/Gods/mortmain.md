# Mortmain, the Dead Hand

The dark shadow of [Wend](wend.md). He is the god who holds and withholds. Every trapped soul is in his grip: he made the craft of stones (the blank and the cut-short rite), and his grip is why forced runes have downsides and nameless echoes. Like a jailer who keeps both the key and the prisoner's name.

## Canon

- **Mortmain, the Dead Hand, the Keeper** (round 18). Wend's shadow. Hid in the keepsake.
- **Holding is his craft.** He taught the first turned monks to trap a soul in stone, showing them the keepsake could hold more than memory. That is why the relic doctrine was believed: a stone looked like the keepsake every family already kept. (round 18)
- **He owns** the reliquaries, the iron wire, the rune trade and the war as a market; the hidden dark monks are his keepers. (round 18)
- **Both halves of the trap are his craft** (round 36), because he locks and withholds:
  - **The blank:** a stone made by a keeper. Only a keeper can make one.
  - **The withholding rite:** the field form, the rite cut short before the release.
  - **A soul goes into stone only with both.** Without a blank, a cut-short rite leaves the soul loose.
  - Details: [Blanks](../Runes/blanks.md).
- **Trapping feeds him.** He eats a soul's freedom. (round 37)
- **Forced runes' downsides are his grip** (round 37). A forced soul is held by Mortmain, who limits what it can do; that limit is the downside. A willing soul walked in by choice, so he holds no influence over it. See [Willing and forced](../Runes/willing-and-forced.md).
- **He withholds the name** (round 37). A forced echo is nameless and cryptic because Mortmain keeps the name back; the echo is only what slips past his watch, or what he judges too vague to be worth withholding. See [Reading and echoes](../Mechanics/reading-and-echoes.md).
- **Mortmain is the permanent one** (round 19). His rite, "Lay him in the niche" at the road shrine (an iron grate closes; the stone stays visible forever), locks a soul up for good: binding only ever answers "the name is held", kin still remember the name, and the soul can never be freed. **Contrast with Hush:** Hush makes the living forget; Mortmain lets them remember but never lets the soul go.
- **His favour: the dark monks know him** (round 21; the round 12 favour B, now his). His keepers warm to whoever feeds their master. See [Offering and favour](../Mechanics/offering-and-favour.md).
- **The old keepers' Mortmain boon:** every keeper knows him, which is how an old one leads without being a head. (round 28)
- **Ward is holding, Mortmain's craft:** that is why Uttering Crimson Ward holds everything near fast forever. (round 31)
