# Grudge, the Kept Coal

The dark shadow of [Gled](gled.md). He is the anger at dying. He eats a soul's refusal to end: a scrap when a rune is uttered, a full meal at his own rite. Men who refuse to fall feed him for years while they live.

## Canon

- **Grudge, the Kept Coal** (round 18). Gled's shadow. Hid in the grief thrown on the pyre: the anger at dying, the quarrel never forgiven, kept alive as a coal. **Grudge is the anger at dying.** (rounds 18–19)
- **What he eats: the refusal,** a soul's anger at its own ending. (round 37) Mortmain eats a soul's freedom, Hush its name, Grudge its refusal.
- **Uttering is his rite turned inside out:** a soul burned for a spell instead of a body burned for release. (round 18)
  - **The soul rages as it burns; Grudge eats the rage as it flares.** Only a scrap of a meal, so uttering still eases withdrawal less than a dark rite. (round 37; rewords round 13's "never a meal")
  - **What he doesn't finish walks off as the hatred Hollow:** the kept coal, his smouldering leftovers. (round 37; the Hollow itself is round 10 canon, see [Hollows](../Characters/hollows.md))
- **His own rite at the pyre is his full meal.** It consumes the soul by twisting it into a Hollow. (round 19, replacing round 18's "feeds on spite, never on souls") Words: "Bank the coal." (round 19) It casts no spell and gives no shards; what the Wanderer gets is the favour. (round 20)
- **His favour: "Not yet."** For a while, a blow that should kill the Wanderer leaves him standing on a sliver of health, once. Relief, not power. Proposed text: "You should have fallen. Something in you refused." (round 19, design canon) It also spares him the death costs. (round 24)
- **"Not yet" feeds him** (round 37): men who refuse to fall burn with anger for years, and he feeds on them while they live. **The Unfallen are his living coals.** See [The Unfallen](../Characters/the-unfallen.md).
- **"Not yet" stops blows, not wasting.** (round 34) The testament king got it at the pyre and still rotted in bed.
- **The old keepers' Grudge boon:** "Not yet" again and again, which is why they are so old. (round 28)
- **He will not let a Crimson prisoner go;** that is one reason no god accepts a Crimson stone. (round 31)

## Open

Proposals (round 37, not decided):
- **Grudge doesn't start wars, he keeps them going.** Battlefields of men dying angry are his feast.
- **The Letting Go starves him:** a soul that dies at peace gives him nothing.
- **Uttering a willing rune raises no Hollow.** Not recommended: it breaks "every utterance raises a Hollow" (round 10).
- **Binding a soul Grudge twisted into a Hollow** (round 20 thread): proposal "nothing answers", as for a hatred Hollow, since his rite consumes the soul. Cost: binding can't tell a Grudge soul from a freed one. Fix if needed: near a fire the binding answers with ember ("Something answered, and it was angry."). Also open: whether a Grudge-rite Hollow has all hatred Hollow traits (recommended yes) and where it rises. The build (round 47, rites.gd) raises it outside the village: "The coal takes him. Past the wall, something gets up." Why outside is open; proposal: it takes the nearest unburned body, and inside the walls the dead are burned (round 39), so the nearest lies beyond the palisade.
