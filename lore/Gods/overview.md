# The Gods: Overview

Six old gods in three pairs. Three good gods (the Patient gods) once carried the dead home through a three-part rite, the good death. Each of the three dark gods (the Quiet gods) hid inside one part of that rite, like a mould growing in the cracks of a house.

## Canon

### All the gods (rounds 6 and 8)

- **All the gods are old.** Some are good, some are dark. Neither side is younger. (round 8)
- **The good gods exist, but are very weak now.** The user calls them "angelic deities"; in-world they are the Patient gods ("angelic" is banned as Berserk's God Hand word).
- **The dark gods hid while the world was pious.** When the world was devout, they were weak and hid so they could go on existing. (round 8)
- **The wars and the corruption made them strong.** Wrongdoing feeds them; they are now very powerful. (rounds 6, 8)
- **Worshipping the dark gods drives you mad,** because they are so ancient. (round 8) **Madness has no owner** among the gods. (round 18)
- **The old rites freed souls.** In ancient times the rite for the dead released the soul to the good gods. (round 18 replaces the round 5 detail that the name came last: the name is now spoken first.)
- **The false rite turned the old rite inside out.** Souls were trapped in stones, and stones were later offered to the dark gods. An offered rune: the name is forgotten, the soul consumed, the rune destroyed.
- **The dark gods corrupted some monks** as they grew. Those monks (the [keepers](../Organizations/keepers.md)) began the false rite.
- **Freeing is still possible:** a rune given back at a [wayside shrine](../Locations/wayside-shrines.md) has its soul freed.
- **The Quiet gods never speak on screen and never bargain.** (rounds 8, 18, 28) Their favour shows only in world text.

### The shape: "Shadows" (P1, round 17; six gods, round 18)

- **Each dark god hid inside one part of the good death, the rite of one good god.** The turned monks kept the same prayers with one part changed, so the false rite spread unseen. (round 17)
- **Three pairs, six gods.** Never five dark gods (God Hand). (round 18)

### The good death (round 18)

1. **The Naming** (Ennick). At the deathbed the monk spoke the true name aloud. Kin tied a knot in a cord for it, and the cord went into the chapel ledger. A named soul can be found and called home. The name comes first. (round 19) In the war, chaplains tied the cord at the dying man's wrist, because no kin were on the field. (round 19)
2. **The Letting Go** (Gled). A soul can't leave while something holds it: its body, or the grief of the living. The named body was burned, and kin each put something of their grief in the fire (a lock of hair, a last word, a quarrel forgiven). When the fire was out, nothing held the soul.
3. **The Walking** (Wend). Kin carried the ashes along the road to a wayside shrine, set them down and turned back without looking. Wend walked the rest of the way. Each family kept one small token of the dead (a ring, a button) as a keepsake.

### What feeds each dark god (decided round 37)

Think of three thieves at one grave, each taking a different thing.

- **Mortmain eats a soul's freedom.** Making the blank and trapping a soul in it are his craft; trapping feeds him.
- **Hush eats its name.** He is fed only when stones are offered to him, as the keepers did. An offered name is forgotten, even by kin.
- **Grudge eats its refusal,** the anger at its own ending. He is fed when a rune is uttered and its soul burned, and fully by his own rite at the pyre.
- This replaces round 9–13's "only a dark rite is a meal; an utterance feeds them only indirectly, as wrongdoing, never as a meal." Uttering is now a scrap of a meal for Grudge. The balance holds: the dark gods still prefer their rites, uttering still eases withdrawal less (round 13), and the dark monks still want the Wanderer to give, not burn. (See [Uttering](../Mechanics/uttering.md).)
- **Wrongdoing in general still strengthens them** (round 6).

### Favour (rounds 10, 12, 21)

- **Offering earns the dark gods' favour,** enough to tempt a player to keep doing it. (round 10) Favour shows through world text, never a god's voice. (round 8)
- **Each dark god's favour has an owner** (round 21): Hush, the stones go quiet; Mortmain, the dark monks know him; Grudge, "Not yet". Details: [Offering and favour](../Mechanics/offering-and-favour.md).

### IP notes kept (round 18)

Avoid five dark gods (God Hand), "Cinder"/"First Flame" fire language (Dark Souls), fire plus madness (Elden Ring's Frenzied Flame), Spanish-Catholic naming (Blasphemous). "Mortmain" is a real legal term, not IP.
