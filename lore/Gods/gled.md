# Gled, the Pyre

The good goddess of the Letting Go. She cuts the ties that keep a soul from leaving. Pyres and firelight are hers. Her dark shadow is [Grudge](grudge.md).

## Canon

- **Gled, the Pyre** (round 18, T1; replaces "the Banked Fire"). Cuts the ties: "What burns is free of the hand." Grudging, stubborn, still doing her work unasked.
- **Pyres, firepits and firelight are hers.** Firelight is the only light besides runes (ART_RULES §1, §3).
- **Burned bodies do not walk,** because nothing is left for a loose soul to hold. (round 18; see [Hollows](../Characters/hollows.md))
- **Her rite:** at the fire: "Give him to the fire." The dark rite there is Grudge's "Bank the coal". (round 19)
- **What burns under her rite is what holds the soul** (a keepsake, a letter), not the stone. (round 20)
- **Someone to let it go:** when kin can't let go, they must burn their keepsake themselves. (round 20)
- **Her buff** (round 21): for a while, Hollows laid down nearby burn and stay down. Practical, never combat power.
- Her step gives no corruption drop; the soul goes free only at Wend's road. (round 20)
- **No fire root name:** fire belongs to Gled and Grudge, and "what burns is free of the hand", so burning cannot be held in stone. (round 21 rule for new root names)
