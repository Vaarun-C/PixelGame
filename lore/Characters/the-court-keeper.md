# The Court Keeper

The keeper who stayed at the dying king's side when the rest left, and gave him the cut-short rite. He is the "him" in "Not him", and the last of the six Crimson prisoners, sealed at the Mother House.

## Canon

- **When the keepers left the king's dying court, one stayed:** the king's own court keeper. (round 35)
- **He gave the king the field form** at his deathbed, because the king was the richest stone in the realm. (round 34)
- **"Him" was this chaplain.** (round 27) He was not the Wanderer.
- **He is the last Crimson prisoner** (round 31): jailer, the king's old servant, the witness; Hunger; the Mother House, and the truth of "Not him". See [The Unfallen](the-unfallen.md).

## Open

- **The king asked a man he knew was a keeper for the true rite** (history review round 36, point 4). He was the keepers' patron, so "knew the man" when the words stopped doesn't hold. Fixes: (a) recommended: **the one who stayed hid even from the king.** The king believed all his keepers had left; this one passed as an honest monk (a young recruit). "Not him" then means "not this one, I trusted this one". (b) The king had no other monk allowed near him; weaker.
- **"All but one" as a pattern:** this leaving is separate from the chapels' fall ~10 years ago; "they always leave before the end" can be a pattern players notice. (round 34 proposal)
