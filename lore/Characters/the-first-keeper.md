# The First Keeper (first boss)

An old, mad keeper who stayed behind, sealed in a chapel in the Chapel Ruins with an empty larder. He is weak and starving, the first boss, and the first person to start telling the Wanderer the truth, in a way easy to dismiss.

## Canon (round 29)

- **One old keeper stayed,** sealed in one hand-made chapel, mad, at the Pull. **He is the first boss, of sorts, and starts revealing the truth.** The other old keepers stay late-game bosses (round 28).
- **He is weak:** ten years alone at the Pull with an empty larder; withdrawal has starved him. His utterances raise only small hatred Hollows.
- **The Wanderer must kill him;** the fight can't be skipped. It teaches the loop: corruption rises for the kill, his stones drop as souls to free, freeing them at a shrine brings it down.
- **He raves the truth** ("I fed them. You'll feed them."; proposed), and the Wanderer can write it off as stone-sickness. A seed, not the mid-arc break (round 25).
- **His face is unremembered** (his Hush boon), so no villager can describe him.
- **He carries a page of the sale book:** the first pointer to the Mother House.
- Ten years since the chapel fell fits TB (chapels fell ~10 years ago). (round 32 note)
