# The Unfallen (the Crimson Prisoners)

Men Grudge would not let die. They kept earning "Not yet" until no blow could kill them, so the only answer was to lock them in stone, each held by a willing jailer. The first were the mad, overpowered guards of the testament king; more came out of the long war. Six are known so far.

## Canon

- **What they are** (round 30): men of the wars, mostly champions, captains and keepers, whom Grudge would not let die. They kept doing dark rites and kept earning "Not yet", until death stopped taking them. Ember shows in wounds that never close. **Every killing blow leaves them standing.**
- **Grudge's living coals** (round 37): men who refuse to fall burn with anger for years, and Grudge feeds on them while they live.
- **The first were the king's guards** (round 37): the strongest of the testament king's guards, **driven mad but immensely powerful from the strong runes they used,** sealed by his inner circle of readers (the Crimson Circle). **So the sealing began in the border wars,** before the testament war, and continued through the long war. (Fixes "the war made them", round 30: begun in the border wars, continued through the long war.)
- **Sealed in Crimson** by a willing jailer: two names, one yes (see [Crimson](../Runes/crimson.md)). **Their names were torn into pieces** and hidden in different ledgers by the circle.
- **Six prisoners for now** (round 31; more may be added; names to be exact game strings later):
  - **The king's champion:** first reader of the testament; jailer, the widow of a man he cut; Rend; the court chapel, and the first pointer to the Mother House. Sealed ~11 years ago (TB).
  - **The Paymaster:** a captain who paid his company in their dead mates' stones, then made more when the silver ran out (from blanks the keepers sold him, round 36). **He is the exception:** every other company drew coin wages, and stones were not pay (round 38b); jailer, his company's war chaplain, who trained beside the Wanderer; Hunger; the Battlefield, and the Wanderer's own past.
  - **The Ford-holder:** a captain who held a river ford for a whole season against every side in turn; jailer, his brother, who fought on the other side; Ward; Rot Swamp (the drowned ford).
  - **The Pass warden:** a keeper who ran the gleaners on the deserters' road and took the deserters too; jailer, a gleaner who carried one stone too many and read it; Ward; Frozen Pass ice tombs.
  - **The Coal-master:** a pyre-master who banked the coals for the crowns, turning the field dead into Hollows to hold ground; Grudge's own; jailer, a village monk of the long rite; Rend; Ashen Forest.
  - **The court keeper (last):** the man who gave the king the false rite; jailer, the king's old servant, the witness; Hunger; the Mother House, and the truth of "Not him". See [The court keeper](the-court-keeper.md).
- **The champion and the widow** (rounds 30–31):
  - **His gear:** his reading gauntlet, stamped with the Mother House's mark for a certified reader (proof the Order sold the readings); worn, a Rend stone reads in fewer cuts.
  - **Her echo:** her husband was cut at the readings; she knew the long rite from burying her father and heard the field form stop over her husband. Proposed: "He cut them until the stone talked. I wanted it to stop talking." Her secret: where the circle met, and the mark they cut on milestones beside "Not him", which leads to the next Crimson stone.
- **Imprisoned things** (design canon): too strong to defeat; released when a Crimson rune is spoken or released, then fought as bosses or mini-bosses. Never bargain. Crimson bosses carry large steps of story. (round 8)
