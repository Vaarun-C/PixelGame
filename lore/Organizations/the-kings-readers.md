# The King's Readers

The testament king's trusted inner circle, who made and ran his stones. Beast stones only work while their reader lives, so they were the people his wealth stood on. When they saw him make stones of prisoners, they understood what stones were, and became the Crimson Circle.

## Canon

- **"A trusted few were his readers."** (round 34) A beast stone works while set and while its reader lives.
- **They were his inner circle** (round 37): a few came from the Order and made the runes; the others put the souls inside (the first tool runes, the beast stones).
- **They learned the truth** when he began making runes from prisoners, and **became the [Crimson Circle](the-crimson-circle.md),** sealing his strongest guards. (round 37)

## Open

- **Who trapped the beasts:** round 34 canon says the keepers did and the readers set them; round 37 says the readers put the souls inside. See [Blanks](../Runes/blanks.md).
- **The famine** if readers died or quit: see [The testament king](../Characters/the-testament-king.md).
- **They were the first to read many stones,** before the relic doctrine was set, so the likeliest to notice; they learned about corruption by watching (men who uttered and the king who did rites wasted; readers who only set stones did not). (round 34 proposal)
