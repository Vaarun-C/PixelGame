# The Keepers (the Dark Monks)

The few monks who secretly serve the dark gods. They make the blanks, wrote the cut-short rite, ran the stone trade, and killed anyone who asked too much. Young ones hide in villages as ordinary monks; the oldest are mad, boon-laden and live in remote places.

## Canon

- **The dark gods corrupted some monks;** those monks began the false rite. Mortmain taught them the craft (blank and withholding rite); they serve Hush and Grudge too. (rounds 6–7, 18, 36)
- **They are Mortmain's keepers.** (round 18) **They make the blanks;** only a keeper can (round 36).
- **They came first:** already hidden in the Order when the testament king found them and became their patron. (round 34)
- **They left the king's dying court** for the Order's houses and began selling stones to everyone. **One stayed** (the [court keeper](../Characters/the-court-keeper.md)). (round 35)
- **They have no leader. They serve the dark gods directly.** (round 28)
- **A few old keepers are so corrupt, for so long, that the gods gave them boons, and they are completely mad.** They lead or brainwash new recruits. (round 28)
  - **They never live inside village walls** (a mad man can't pass as a tired monk). They keep to remote places (a sealed chapel, a hermitage, the Mother House undercroft). **The dark monks in villages are the young, lucid ones.**
  - **Boons are the three favours, deepened:** Hush, their own stones always quiet and a face no one remembers; Mortmain, every keeper knows him; Grudge, "Not yet" again and again (why they are so old). Boons follow rites and are never negotiated; no god speaks.
  - **They live at the Pull:** madness brings withdrawal, so they must keep doing dark rites. That is why they ran the trade, why they recruit, and why, with the war's supply gone, they want the Wanderer's stones. (answers round 16 hole 6, partly)
  - **They are late-game bosses** (round 28), except the [first keeper](../Characters/the-first-keeper.md) (round 29). A keeper at the Pull fights with strong utterances, each raising a hatred Hollow. His stones drop as souls to free. He may tempt but never deals for a god. Killing him costs corruption; freeing his stones earns it back.
- **They kill only to protect the secret** (stone-sickness, round 25), never to fill stones.
- **They want the Wanderer to offer, not burn.** (round 9)
- **They lie on rite cords by swapping names,** to the clean only. (round 15)
- **They know an offerer** (Mortmain's favour since round 21): warmer, cheaper carving, a name for binding; subtle at low corruption, plain near madness. Once known, he can offer through one; any dark rite for metal. (rounds 12, 19)
- **They cleared the chapels' larder** and left with the sale book (round 29). See [Chapel Ruins](../Locations/chapel-ruins.md).
