# Reading and Echoes

A rune gives its full power only once you "read" it, by doing what it names. Reading also gives the soul's echo, a short cryptic line. Forced echoes are nameless because Mortmain keeps the name back. The Order explained echoes away as "the track a life left", and killed men who asked too many questions.

## Canon

### Reading (design canon, not built)

- **A rune must be read before it gives its full power.** You read it by doing what it names (Rend: cutting; Ward: taking blows; Hunger: drinking from the wounded). **Reading is personal;** the stone can change hands.
- **Using leaked; making stayed secret** (round 36): rivals learned to read seized stones this way.

### Reading and the relic doctrine (round 25)

- **The doctrine admits what the echo says and denies anyone is there.** The echo is the track a life left in its name: true about the man, but he has gone free. Doctrine lines: "A ring remembers the hand. It is not the hand." / "The track points where the foot was going. The foot is gone." / "A man would answer. A stone repeats."
- **Forced echoes are fixed and nameless, the same for every reader.** Kin, places and wants can be in the line; finding a widow proved the man had lived, not that he was in the stone.
- **Why they are nameless and cryptic: Mortmain withholds the name** (round 37). The echo is only what slips past his watch, or what he judges too vague to be worth withholding. (Replaces round 25's "taken in Hush's silence" and drops the round 36 Ennick proposal.)
- **A name comes back only to the man who spoke it at the rite** (recognition, not revelation; fits round 10). Stones he did not shrive give him only the echo; their names are found in the world.
- **The Wanderer's first reading** (round 38): at the Pull, ~3 years ago, with the Rend stone lashed to his ricasso by its own wire tail (round 39), cutting until it read. "I know this name. I said it." See [The Wanderer](../Characters/the-wanderer.md).
- **How the name comes back at the Pull** (round 38b): reading still works at the Pull (it is doing what the rune names); only the echo stops. Reading puts the reader in touch with the soul. For anyone else a forced stone stays nameless; the name comes back to the Wanderer only because he spoke this exact man's name in the field rite. Plain memory without reading gives nothing, and memory is still not a binding source. The soul's need is separate and comes later. No conflict with round 10.
- **The chaplains' rule: "A shriving hand holds no stone."** Chaplains, the only ones who could recognise a name, never read one. (Still true with blanks: the dying man held the stone, round 36.)
- **Willing stones speak their own name, plainly, to any reader** (Mortmain has no grip on them, round 37). They were few, bare and rarely read. **The people who noticed were the Crimson Circle** (round 25; the Circle itself began as the king's readers, round 37).
- **An echo changes only when someone finishes part of the soul's need.** In the kingdom era no one ever did, so no one saw a stone answer. That is what breaks the doctrine for the Wanderer.
- **Echoes go silent while Hush's favour quiets a stone** (round 37) and **stop at the Pull** (round 20). **The Whisper can mishear a wish, never a name** (round 20).
- **UI:** a stone's line is labelled "Echo:" (the order's word); once the man's name is known, it becomes his name. (design canon)
- **The echo pool** (round 44, being built): common forced echoes live in `godot/scripts/game/echoes.json`, 8 lines per root name, picked by the stone's soul id so each stone's line stays fixed. Each line carries the dying act of its family (refusing, clinging, slipping away). This is game data: the lines are claims of the dead, not canon facts. Authored notable stones (the Pale Rend, Crimson, bosses) get their own lines, not pool lines. (seen in: echoes.json)

### Stone-sickness (round 25)

- **The Order named it a wasting madness from listening to relics,** so the deaths that followed were expected. It is a cover.
- **A man who asked too many of the right questions was "sent to the chaplains":** taken into the Order's care, where he died, got the false rite, and his stone went back into the trade. The soldiers who handed him over suspected nothing.
- **The one narrow exception to "dark monks trap the dying, they don't murder":** they kill only to protect the secret, never to fill stones; the deaths must look natural.

## Open

- **Stone-sickness knock-ons** (proposals): ledger entries for stone-sick men all shriven by the same few hands, a clue; their stones can turn up in his bag; old soldier: "Asked too much of his stones. Wasted away. They said it would."
- **The king's guard were the first real stone-sick** (round 34 proposal): withering from uttering and dark rites; the Order later used the word as a cover. "A lie sits best on a truth." Line: "The king's guard went first. Wasted in their beds. Stone-sick, the monks said. That time they were right."
- **Other closures, not confirmed:** most stones were graded by sight and never read (glow sets the price); readers hear the line in their own inner voice, so kin couldn't know a voice; stones were money, so no one left them at shrines.
- **Proposed sayings:** soldiers: "Read it. Never answer it." / "Stones mutter. So do drunks."; the rite's teaching: "The name is said, the soul goes, the stone keeps the silence."; barterer today: "Not at my stall."
- **Alternatives weighed:** "the stone mirrors the reader's guilt" (breaks on two readers hearing the same words; folk belief at most); "everyone knew and didn't care" (contradicts trust in the rite); "the dead fight beside you" (heroic drift).
- **IP flag:** "echo" brushes Bloodborne's Blood Echoes (a currency); low risk since ours is never money. Soldier slang "the mutter" is an alternative.
- **The Pale Rend's echo as a later beat** (round 38 proposal): "He said my name. Then nothing." is heard the first time he reads a stone below the Pull.
- **Pool repeats** (round 44 flag): with 8 lines per verb, many stones share an echo, which weakens "finding a widow proved the man had lived". Proposed fixes: (a) men die alike in a war, so a shared line is in-world ("Half the field says that."); (b) build an echo from two short fragments (act + detail) to multiply the pool; (c) only clue-bearing stones carry kin or places, pool lines stay generic.
- **Withheld:** whether the Order's founders believed the doctrine.

## Rejected

- **Forced echoes nameless because the name went into Ennick's count** (round 36 proposal). Dropped round 37 for Mortmain withholding it.
