# The Living Binding

The worst act in the game: binding a living person's name into a stone against their will. It can only be done as a dark rite, and the victim is left an empty husk. Most of the details are still proposals.

## Canon (round 21; design canon, not built)

- **A living person's name can be bound without consent, but only as a rite, never as a plain binding.**
- **It is a dark rite in its own right,** beside the three dark rites of the six. **The victim goes hollow.**
- **It carries the heaviest corruption cost of any act.** (the user's direction; amount is tuning)
- **A dark monk's swapped cord name can name a living villager (C2).** (C2 held since round 15 is released.)
- **Supersedes round 5's "only a living volunteer":** a plain binding takes a living name only with consent. (See [Rebuilding and binding](../Runes/rebuilding-and-binding.md).)

## Open (proposals, round 21; ranked 5 in the old list)

- **Why a plain binding can't take the living:** "A soul can't leave while something holds it: its body" (the Letting Go). A plain binding of a living name fails: **"Something living holds him."** A volunteer lets go himself; that is consent.
- **Owner: Mortmain** (recommended). Holding is his craft; the rite swaps the body's hold for his hand. **The one dark rite with no good twin:** the good death never took the living. Not Hush (kin keep the name) and not Grudge (no anger, no fire). Round 37 fit: trapping feeds Mortmain.
- **Rite name and words:** "the Taking in Hand"; prompt **"Take him in hand."** In the Whisper: "Give him rest."
- **Place:** Mortmain's niche at a road shrine. The empty rebuilt stone is set in the niche, **wound with something the living person owns and holds dear** (a ring, a lock of hair, a knife); he speaks the name and the words; the grate shuts and opens. Text: "The grate opens. The stone is warm." The victim isn't there; binding calls a name, not a body.
- **The thing of his is the gate:** he must get it from the victim or kin, a quest and a lie told to a living person.
- **The stone:** forced (wired), tier from the living name (villagers give Pale or Azure); echo is a living man's ("I was at my hearth."). **Recommended: freeable only after the empty body is burned** (Gled, with the kin's consent to burn someone still breathing), then Wend's road. Alternative: permanent like the niche (simpler; no way to atone).
- **Corruption:** heaviest, **flat, not scaled by tier.** Freeing later lowers it only as a normal freeing does.
- **How it is learned: fooled once, through C2.** A dark monk gives a swapped name; the binding answers "Something living holds him." Monk: "He clings. Bring me something of his, and we'll help him go." The Wanderer fetches the ring from a woman who thinks she's helping her husband's dead brother; the monk gives the words. Back in the village, the man sits by the firepit, empty. The cord, checked in a ledger, names someone else.
- **After that, C2 is a clue, not a trap:** a plain binding of a swapped living name answers "Something living holds him", which tells him the monk lied. He can refuse; only the first time costs a villager.
- **The village remembers:** the victim is a **husk** (breathes, eats if fed, never speaks, sits where left). Kin feed him. When he dies he must be burned or a loose soul may take the body inside the walls. Whether the village knows who did it depends on what they saw. Lines: peasant: "He eats if you put it in his mouth." / guard: "Last stranger who asked after a man's ring. Look where he sits." The dark monk warms to him.
- **Weak spots and fixes:** farming villagers (heavy flat cost, faint names, a quest each, a village that remembers); online griefing (own world only for now); tone (never a power move; offered only by dark monks in soft words).
- **Round 5 clash:** binding source (c) "rarely, a living volunteer" stays; only "volunteer only" is superseded.
