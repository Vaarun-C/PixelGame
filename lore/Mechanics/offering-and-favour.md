# Offering and Favour

Offering is giving a rune to Hush: the glow sinks, the name is forgotten even by the dead man's family, and the soul is gone. It costs more corruption than an utterance, but the dark gods pay it back in favour, which is what makes it tempting.

## Canon (round 12; owners round 21; design canon, not built)

### Where he can offer

- **Below 75%:** only at a **spent reliquary** in the Chapel Ruins (the old larder; the lid closes on the light).
- **From the Whisper (75%+):** also **anywhere,** as a verb in the bag.
- **Through a dark monk,** once he knows the monk is one. That monk can perform any of the three dark rites for him, for a price in metal; that is how Mortmain's keepers make a living. (round 19)

### What it does

- **How it looks:** no flash, no voice. The glow sinks inward, the stone greys, the wire falls loose. (Proposed text: "The wire came away in your hand." Loose Wire: "Nothing left to hold.")
- **Name forgotten, soul consumed, rune destroyed.** This is Hush's meal; he feeds only on names (round 37).
- **Cost:** more corruption than an utterance, and no shards back. Both scale with tier.
- **No Hollow rises.** The name is forgotten before the soul can hate.
- **Eases withdrawal at the Pull more than uttering.** (round 13)

### Favour (the user chose B and C from the round 11 menu)

- **Each dark god's favour has an owner** (round 21): **C** (the stones go quiet) is Hush's and follows an offering. **B** (the dark monks know him) is Mortmain's and follows his rite. Grudge's is "Not yet" (see [Grudge](../Gods/grudge.md)).
- **(C) The stones go quiet** (round 12; re-grounded round 37):
  - When the Wanderer offers a rune, Hush also tastes the names of the forced souls he carries. For a while they forget who they were, so they stop straining against Mortmain's grip, and **their downsides ease.**
  - **While quiet, their echoes go silent too,** so their needs can't be learned until it wears off. (round 37)
  - **Only runes up to the offered rune's tier go quiet.** (rounds 12–13)
  - **Crimson never goes quiet:** its downside is the prisoner's will, not a soul remembering its name. (round 37)
- **(B) The dark monks know him.** Only dark monks react: a warmer line, cheaper carving, a name for binding. Ordinary monks notice nothing. The hunter has to feed his prey to find it.
- **Strength and length scale with the tier given.**
- **Favour text is always true, even in the Whisper.** The dark gods don't need to lie about their gifts. (round 13)
- **The hints scale with corruption** (the user's rule): subtle at low corruption, plain near madness. Proposed examples:
  - Quiet stone, low: "The wire is cold." / near 100%: "The wire is cold. The man inside has stopped asking."
  - Dark monk, low: a look held a moment too long; the carving costs a few grams less and he says nothing. / near 100%: "They remember who feeds them."
- **Offering doesn't change who brings him back from death** (round 12; see [Death](death.md)).

## Open

- **Place clash:** Hush's rite is also at the ledger ("Scrape his name", round 19), but the offering is at a spent reliquary. Proposal: both; the reliquary is where the keepers fed stones to Hush (the larder), the ledger where a name is unwritten. Or move the offering to the ledger.
- **Consequence of the round 21 owners, to confirm:** round 12 said offering is "the one sure way to learn which monk is dark". With B now Mortmain's, the sure way is laying a soul in his niche. Proposal: accept; the first niche (fooled once) opens the dark monks to him.
