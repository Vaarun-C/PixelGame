# Death

When the Wanderer dies he comes back in a village. While his corruption is low, Wend carries him home, but only the man: his bag stays where he fell. Once he is deep in corruption, the dark gods bring him back with everything, and quietly take one rune as their price.

## Canon

- **On death:** "You have fallen." He comes back in a village. (seen in: main.js; respawns at the last village entered, south side; today the bag and socketed runes are kept; "lashed" in canon since round 39) (round 9)
- **Who sends him back depends on corruption** (round 10): while it is low, the good gods spend what little they have (**Wend**, round 21); from the Whisper (75%), the dark gods won't let him go, because they still want his offerings. **The death text changes** with them. (design canon; today the text is always "You have fallen")

### What death costs (round 24, design canon, not built)

- **Low corruption: the road carries only the man.** The dead carry nothing on Wend's road (why kin lay coin on the eyes, round 23). What he wears and wields comes back: sword, plate, lashed runes. What he carries stays where he fell: the bag, with metal, loose runes, shards and scrap. He walks back to fetch it. Proposed text: "The road brought you home. It brought nothing else."
- **Dying again before he reaches the bag:** a nearby loose-soul Hollow picks it up and carries it (a risen corpse carries what it had, round 15); kill that Hollow to get it back. Hatred Hollows never take it (no body). With no Hollow nearby, the bag stays where it lies. **The bag is never destroyed.**
- **From the Whisper on: the dark gods let go of nothing,** so the bag comes back with him. **But they take one rune per death,** at random from bag and lashed stones together. It is taking, not bargaining. Proposed text (to be reworded to cover a lashed stone): "They would not let you go. Something you carried is quieter."
  - The stone is consumed as an offering (glow sinks, stone greys, wire falls loose) but **adds no corruption, because he didn't choose it.**
  - **They take only what is on his person.** A bag left in the field is safe; lashed runes can still be taken. (The user wrote "if the bag is not with me, nothing gets taken"; read as "nothing from the field bag".)
- **Grudge's "Not yet" cheats a death,** so it spares him these costs.
- Offering does not change who returns him (round 12).
