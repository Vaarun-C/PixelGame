# Fixed world map: design

Replace the endless procedural world (new seed each load) with one fixed, hand-editable land: the
Wake, seed-23 horseshoe, ~2000 × 2000 tiles (~18 min walk edge to edge). This first version makes
the agreed layout walkable with placeholder art. Gates, shortcuts and keys are decided later by
playing it.

## What goes in (agreed in the map sessions)

- **Outline:** the seed-23 horseshoe around the Black Mere; the north edge is the White Teeth wall;
  the Grey (deep water) beyond every shore.
- **Elevation:** five land levels (1 lowland … 5 mountain), layout "court hill + isle peak".
- **Vegetation zones:** fen (Rot Swamp), the Long Field (battlefield), fallow fields, dead woods,
  court grounds + chapel ruins, frost pines, bare mountain, Ashen Forest, Cinder peak.
- **Water:** the full meandering river and stream network, the upland lake, the Court Hill pools
  and the Pass tarn.
- **Roads:** the King's Road, the Pass road, the East road (with the causeway to Ashholm) and the
  Low road, winding on slopes.
- **Places:** villages Greywell (start), Ravengate, Blackford, Ashbarrow, Thornstead; the six
  Crimson prisoners' places (markers only); Court Chapel, Mere Chapel, first keeper's chapel; the
  Mother House site; the crossroads; seven wild shrines; the prologue field (marker only).
- **Walls:** cliffs and slopes by the agreed rules (lowland ↔ plain slope; plain ↔ upland
  escarpment with gaps; anything ↔ highland/mountain cliff; roads always ramp), dense wood cores,
  deep water and rivers (crossed at bridges and the causeway), mountains except on the Pass road.

The design scripts and their PNGs move from the scratchpad into `tools/world_design/` so the
layout can be regenerated or tweaked before it is baked.

## How it is built

### 1. Design export (Python, one-off)

`tools/world_design/export.py` scales the 500 px design (1 px = 4 tiles) up to tile resolution
(2001 × 2001 tile corners), adding fine noise so borders don't show the 4-tile steps, and writes:

- `terrain.png`: terrain id per corner (biome × 4 + ground, as `Terrain.terrain_at` returns).
- `level.png`: level 0–5 per corner (0 = water).
- `ramp.png`: corners where a level edge is walkable (slopes and road ramps).
- `places.json`: villages (name, centre), shrines, chapels, prisoners, crossroads, Mother House,
  start point.

### 2. Region scenes (Godot headless tool, one-off)

`godot/tools/build_world.gd` reads the export and writes 64 region scenes,
`godot/world/regions/r_<x>_<y>.tscn`, each 250 × 250 tiles:

- **Paint layers** (TileMapLayers on a flat swatch tileset, one colour per value): `Terrain`,
  `Level`, `Ramp`. These are what you paint in the Godot editor; they are hidden in play.
- **Props:** every prop as a placed `Prop` node (kind, variant), rolled once by the existing
  spawn rules (`Terrain.prop_at` and biome tables) against the exported terrain. Dense wood cores
  get a closed stand of trees.
- **Places:** marker nodes for villages, shrines, chapels and the other landmarks.

Region scenes are the source of truth once built; the tool is not rerun over hand edits.

### 3. Runtime

- **`WorldMap`** (new, `scripts/world/world_map.gd`): loads region scenes lazily as the player
  nears them and answers `terrain(x, y)`, `level(x, y)`, `ramp(x, y)` and the props and places in
  a chunk.
- **`Terrain`** reads terrain from `WorldMap` instead of noise. The seed is fixed, so decals and
  loot rolls repeat. Ground still draws with the existing dual-grid streaming (`world.gd`, 8-tile
  chunks), so the blended look is unchanged.
- **Cliffs:** each chunk adds collision along level edges that aren't ramps, and draws a cliff face
  on the lower side (3/4 view: a face below upper ground to the north, a rim on the other sides).
- **Water:** deep water and rivers are solid, except where a road crosses (a bridge prop, or the
  causeway to Ashholm); streams and shallows slow the walker like mud.
- **Villages** are generated by the existing `Villages` layout code at the authored centres
  (replacing `centre_in`'s random cells); their ground override and people work as now.
- **Hollow spawning** (`main.gd`) only picks reachable, walkable spots: not water, not cliff-locked
  ledges, not wood cores.
- **Start:** the game starts at Greywell. The prologue scene is unchanged.
- **Playtest tool:** `village:` uses the authored villages; add `place:NAME` to jump to a landmark.

### 4. Placeholder art (via the existing generators, checked with `check_art.py`)

- Ground sheets for four new biomes (fallow fields, dead woods, bare mountain, Cinder rock) plus a
  water biome (deep, river, shallow) and a road ground, in the existing HD ground-sheet format, added
  to `tiles/ground.tres` by `build_tileset.gd`.
- Cliff face, cliff rim and ramp tiles; a bridge prop.
- All colours from `art/palette/master.gpl`. Real PixelLab art replaces them region by region later.

## Not in this version

- Gates, keys, rune gates, one-way drops and shortcuts (decided after walking the map).
- New behaviour at the places (Crimson prisoners, Mother House, chapels): markers only.
- Saving searched loot across sessions (it stays per session, as now).
- A painted preview in the editor; the paint layers show flat swatch colours.

## Testing

- `export.py` asserts every place sits on walkable ground and every village is reachable from
  Greywell by the same walk check used in the design.
- A headless Godot check loads every region scene and confirms the layers cover 2001 × 2001 corners.
- Playtests (screenshots) at Greywell, the crossroads, a Court Hill switchback, a river bridge, the
  Ashholm causeway and a cliff edge; the art-reviewer agent reviews the placeholder art.
- `rune_check.gd` still passes.
